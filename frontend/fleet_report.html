How one operating day is calculated

  1. Vehicles in service = available vehicles − breakdowns, capped by the drivers present.
  2. Capacity per vehicle = (480 min − traffic delay) ÷ time per delivery.
  3. Daily capacity against demand gives the backlog, the share of on-time deliveries and the vehicles required.
  4. Distance × consumption × price gives fuel cost; breakdowns × (duration × rate + parts) give maintenance cost; the backlog gives overtime.
